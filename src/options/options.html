3 Your sites

Nimbus recognises Bitbucket Cloud, GitHub, GitLab and Jira Cloud out of the box — grant page access below to use them. Add self-hosted instances too: include the full URL and any sub-path, e.g. https://corp.example/jira.

Granting page access lets Nimbus recognise pages on that site without you opening the panel first. It never changes where Nimbus can send data — that stays your local gateway only. Surface automatically goes one step further: on a page Nimbus has indexed, a small cue appears in the corner so you don't have to open the panel to find out. Nothing runs until you click it.

4 Where your data goes

One destination. Nimbus only ever sends data to your local gateway. There is no cloud service and no analytics. Page access, granted in stage 3, is a separate axis — it lets Nimbus read pages you allow, but it never changes where anything is sent.

Page access is separate, and it is yours to give. Nimbus can currently read the contents of pages on: no sites yet. Nothing is granted until you grant it, and revoking is one click in stage 3.

What we send, and when. Clipping from the toolbar shows you the whole payload — title, URL, tags, and the body text — before it is sent, and sends nothing until you confirm. The hotkey and the right-click menu stay one gesture: they send straight away and the toast reports the outcome afterwards. Asking Nimbus to fetch an item names that item — the service, the type, and the address — before your gateway reaches out for it.

The secret stays secret. Your pairing token lives in extension storage, is never shown on this page, never written to a log, and never placed in any web page.
